Where the money goes

Wood, paint, hinges, and weatherproofing for new library boxes. Postage to send magazines to libraries that are running low. Small repair kits for boxes that take a beating from weather and time.

This project isn't a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit, so donations here aren't tax-deductible — just a direct way to help keep local libraries stocked.
