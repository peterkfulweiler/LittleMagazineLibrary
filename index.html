Why print still matters

A magazine doesn't buffer, log your location, or disappear when the wifi drops. It sits on a coffee table, gets dog-eared, gets passed from one set of hands to the next, and asks nothing of you but your attention. Every issue in a Free Magazine Library once belonged to somebody — a doctor's waiting room, a grandmother's side table, a college dorm — and now it belongs to whoever needs it next.

Modeled after the Little Free Library movement, every box on this map runs on the same simple rule: take what you want, leave what you can, and let a little bit of paper keep doing what screens can't. Print media isn't a relic. It's a neighborhood.
