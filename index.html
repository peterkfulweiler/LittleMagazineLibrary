Why print still matters

In this era of the endless algorithmic scroll, the print magazine is a countercultural sanctuary of attention. Magazine and newsstands have disappeared across the country, and it is common for large American cities to have no place to buy a print magazine. But it doesn’t have to be this way.

Introducing: The Free Magazine Library. A new community hub where, through the small act of taking a magazine and leaving a magazine, we can help revive print media and create the culture we want to live in. The first Free Magazine Library was built in the print media desert of New Haven, CT, in August 2026. We hope it is the first in a movement. To get started building your own Free Magazine Library, donate to the cause, have your library placed on our official map, or request a micro-grant (up to $50) for supplies, reach out to founders Oliver Egger and Peter Fulweiler at freemagazinelibrary@gmail.com

Modeled after the Little Free Library movement, every box on this map runs on the same simple rule: take what you want, leave what you can, and let a little bit of paper keep doing what screens can't. Print media isn't a relic. It's a neighborhood.
